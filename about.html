Technology & trading flow

MemePerp connects a chain-aware market interface to external quote sources, wallet identity and trading controls. The interface brings those pieces together; the current stress-test simulator records trading outcomes in practice accounts.

01
Market discovery

Search token markets on the selected network. External data providers supply prices and market details; quote availability and eligibility checks determine which markets can be used.

02
Wallet-aware accounts

A compatible wallet identifies the account and network. In the EVM stress-test flow, a signed message proves account control and a separate message confirms each account-changing action.

03
Position controls

Set a side, margin, leverage and order type, then monitor open positions, pending orders and P&L. The stress-test engine applies its own margin, fee, trigger and liquidation calculations.

04
Account history

The terminal shows balances, positions and activity for the selected account. Stress-test results are simulated and do not prove that a live venue could offer the same fill or price.

05

Wallets & signatures

Connecting identifies the account you want to practice with. It does not mean the simulator has custody of your assets. On EVM networks, the interface uses signed messages for sign-in and for confirming actions that change the simulated ledger.

What you may be asked to sign

PromptPurpose in the EVM simulator
Sign-in messageProves control of the connected address for a temporary application session. The message explicitly states that no transaction or funds will be transferred.
Action confirmationConfirms the particular simulated action and its details, such as order side, margin, leverage, order type, or position-management request.
Blockchain transactionEVM simulated orders do not require an on-chain transaction. If a wallet presents an unexpected transaction prompt, stop and inspect it rather than assuming it is part of an EVM simulator order.

Message signing does not move tokens, but always read wallet prompts before approving them. EVM sign-in sessions are temporary; reconnect and sign in again if a session has expired. The Solana testnet uses a separate wallet path and may show testnet-specific prompts.

06

Test balances

EVM practice credits

On Robinhood Chain and BNB Smart Chain, “Add test balance” credits a server-side simulated ledger in ETH or BNB units. The amount is converted using the current collateral quote. No ETH or BNB is deposited, withdrawn, bridged, or transferred by this EVM balance action.

The EVM simulator currently accepts additions worth $1–$1,000 per request, with a maximum of $1,000 in test balance per six-hour window on each selected chain. These are app limits, not wallet funding instructions.

Solana test balance

The Solana tab is a separate testnet flow and displays SOL-denominated test balance. Its “Add test balance” action requests test SOL through the Solana testnet path; testnet tokens are not mainnet SOL and have no intended monetary value. Confirm which network your wallet is using before continuing.

Displayed USD values are estimates. The application converts the simulated native-unit balance using an external ETH or BNB price quote. Quote availability, rounding, and market movement can affect the displayed figure.

Orders & triggers

Perpetual positions let you practice taking a long or short view with simulated leverage. An order is a request interpreted by the simulator—not an instruction submitted to a real exchange.

Immediate quote

Market

Requests a simulated opening using the latest available market quote. The quote may change or fail to load; this is not guaranteed execution at the displayed price.

EVM: market open action
Wait for a level

Limit

A pending EVM order checks its trigger against fetched market data. A long limit is eligible at or below its trigger; a short limit at or above it.

EVM: long ≤ trigger · short ≥ trigger
Wait for a move

Stop

Available for EVM pending orders. A long stop is eligible at or above its trigger; a short stop at or below it. A trigger is not a promise of a fill at that price.

EVM: long ≥ trigger · short ≤ trigger

To leave a limit order pending, its price must be below the current quote for a long or above it for a short. The app rejects a limit that could fill immediately; use Market if you want to open now. Pending EVM orders reserve simulated margin and an estimated opening fee. They may be cancelled while pending; cancellation releases the reserved ledger amount. Orders expire after seven days. Automatic checks run periodically, and an unavailable or ineligible quote will not trigger a simulated fill.

The Solana interface has its own order implementation. Do not assume EVM order rules, limits, or trigger timing apply identically to the Solana path.

08

Positions & margin

A position view can show side, entry and mark prices, margin, leverage, unrealized P&L, and a liquidation estimate. For EVM accounts these are simulator calculations over external quotes and a simulated ledger; they are not a broker statement or on-chain position.

Isolated
In the EVM simulator, the position’s own practice margin is used in its margin calculation. A simulated loss is bounded by the posted margin in the ledger’s isolated-close calculation.
Cross
The EVM simulator can include that chain-and-wallet account’s available practice balance and other cross-position equity in its simulated cushion. It does not draw funds from another wallet or network.
Leverage
Magnifies the simulated notional exposure relative to margin and also magnifies the effect of price changes on ROI. EVM position and order endpoints currently allow 1×–20×.
Liquidation
A modelled threshold based on maintenance margin and the latest available quote. It is an approximation, not a real liquidation process or a guaranteed warning price.

EVM simulated accounts currently allow up to ten open positions and pending orders combined. The app can reject a second position or pending order for the same token until the existing one is closed or cancelled.

On the EVM simulator, TP/SL values are expressed as ROI thresholds. The backend accepts take-profit ROI above zero up to 1,000% and stop-loss ROI below zero down to −100%. These settings are monitoring rules, not guaranteed execution or protection.

10

Markets & pricing

Market discovery and quotes are provided by external market-data services, not generated by an order book operated by MemePerp. The EVM market lookup selects a token pair on the chosen chain and applies basic eligibility checks.

EVM market eligibility: token markets currently need at least $50,000 in reported liquidity and $100,000 in reported market capitalization to be used. Robinhood Chain’s ETH reference is an exception: it uses an Ethereum WETH/USD reference quote because the product does not use a fabricated Robinhood ETH pair.

Token pair quotes can be missing, stale, inconsistent, or temporarily unavailable. EVM collateral quotes are also external and required for converting ETH or BNB collateral to USD. When a quote cannot be obtained, the app may decline an action, show incomplete values, or skip an automatic check rather than fill using a made-up quote.

Risks & limitations

Perpetuals and leverage are high risk even when exploring the mechanics in a simulator. Practice outcomes cannot reproduce every market or operational condition.

Leverage magnifies lossesA small price move against a leveraged position can materially reduce its simulated equity and may reach the modelled liquidation threshold.
Quotes are not execution pricesExternal prices can lag, gap, or differ from prices available elsewhere. The simulator does not guarantee a fill, price, or execution time.
Automation depends on availabilityPending orders and TP/SL checks need usable market and collateral quotes. Delays, provider outages, or an ineligible market can prevent an expected trigger.
Simulations omit real-world market mechanicsDisplayed results do not establish real liquidity, funding conditions, slippage, counterparty access, or the ability to trade a token in a live venue.
Test credits are not assetsEVM practice balances are application ledger entries. They are not redeemable, withdrawable, transferable, or claims on ETH, BNB, or another asset.
Not a security or audit claimThis guide makes no claim that the application, wallet integrations, data sources, or any smart contract have been independently audited or are risk-free.
Use the simulator to learn the interface—not to decide that a live strategy is safe. Nothing on this page is investment, legal, or tax advice. Never sign a wallet request you do not understand.

Quick answers

Will an EVM practice order move funds from my wallet?

No. Robinhood Chain and BNB Smart Chain stress-test actions operate on a simulated account ledger. Wallet message signatures confirm sign-in or the specific requested action; the simulator does not transfer wallet funds for these orders or balance credits.

Why does the wallet ask me to sign?

The signature associates you with the selected account or confirms the details of a simulated action. EVM prompts are signed messages, not order transactions. Review the text in the wallet before signing; reject unexpected prompts.

Does a trigger guarantee a fill?

No. A trigger is evaluated against available quote data. Market movement, price-source delays, eligibility checks, or service availability can affect whether and when the simulator processes it.

Can I withdraw or redeem a test balance?

No. EVM test balances exist only in the simulated ledger and cannot be withdrawn, redeemed, or transferred. Solana test balances are test tokens on Solana Testnet, not mainnet assets.

Is this mainnet trading?

MemePerp’s current trading experience is a stress-test simulator designed to help us prepare for a high inflow of users and deliver a smooth experience. Access to liquidity-backed mainnet trading will be released in batches. Sign up for early beta access.
